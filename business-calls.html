📓 Roofer script & pushback playbook
Opener (15 seconds)
"Hi, this is Scott with Infinity Digital, here in Westminster. Quick question and I'll get out of your way — when a storm rolls through and the phones blow up, what happens to the calls you can't pick up while your guys are on a roof?"
Then do the math with them
"What's an average job worth to you? ... So if three calls a week go to voicemail and the homeowner just calls the next roofer, that's real money walking out the door."
The demo (the whole point of the call)
"We set up an AI receptionist named Ava. She answers every call 24/7, gets the address and the damage, books the inspection, and texts you the details. Don't take my word for it — can I give you the number so you can call her yourself? Then let's grab 15 minutes this week and I'll show you what it'd sound like for your company."
Bridge (only if it fits the card's talking points)
Website gaps (no booking, no chat, dated site)
"I also took a look at your site — I can put together a free mockup of what it'd look like with online booking. No cost to look."
Hiring / fleet / big crew
"Looks like you're growing — we also do branded crew shirts, hoodies and hats. Crews in branded gear on a roof is free advertising to the whole street."
Pushback
"We already have an answering service."
"Good — does it book the inspection, or just take a message? Call Ava and compare. If yours is better, keep it."
"I answer my own phone."
"That's great. What about when you're on a roof, or at 9pm? Ava only picks up the ones you miss."
"How much is it?"
"Depends on call volume — that's what the 15 minutes is for. I'd rather show you first so you can decide if it's worth anything."
"Send me an email."
"Happy to. What's the best email? And so it's not just another email — can I put 15 minutes on the calendar for Thursday?" Then mark Call back.
"Not interested."
"No problem at all — thanks for the time." Mark Not interested. If they say don't call again, mark DNC.
Gatekeeper
"Hi, this is Scott with Infinity Digital — is the owner around? It's about the calls that come in after hours." If not: "When's the best time to catch them?" Log it with Call back and the date.
Voicemail
"Hi, this is Scott with Infinity Digital in Westminster. I've got a quick idea on catching the calls you miss when you're on a job. Give me a call back at [your number]. Thanks."
Rules
No cold texts to these numbers — calls only (TCPA). Never mention the mortgage side. No promises on rankings, revenue or "guaranteed" results. If they say stop, mark DNC on the spot.
